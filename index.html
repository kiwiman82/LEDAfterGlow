From your phone to your shelf in three steps

  1. Send your photo

    Pick one you love: a portrait, a pet, a place. Sharp photos with clear light and shade give the best detail.

  2. We make it

    Your photo is turned into a map of thicknesses and made into white plastic piece by piece.

  3. Switch on a light

    Put the tower over the light and the photo appears. Switch the light off and it goes back to plain white.

Questions

Which photos work best?

Sharp photos with clear light and dark areas. Portraits with the face turned toward the camera, pets and landscapes all work well. Very dark or blurry photos lose detail. If you're not sure, send the best one you have and we'll help you pick the right photo.

Do I need to buy a light?

Not with the complete lithophane. It comes with a wooden base and a 3W LED puck light. If you order the tower on its own, place it over any steady LED light. Avoid hot bulbs, because the plastic can warm up.

How big is it?

About the size of a 1 litre carton. It sits nicely on a shelf or desk, and looks lovely with any home decor.

Why does it look blank when the light is off?

The photo is stored as changes in thickness, so it only shows when light passes through. Unlit, you'll see faint ripples and shading in the white plastic.

What's the difference between glow-in-the-dark and plain white?

Both show your photo when the light is on. The glow-in-the-dark option also gives off a soft glow in a dark room after the light has been on. Glow-in-the-dark adds $10.

How long will it take, and what does postage cost?

Allow about 1 week from the day we confirm your order to the day it arrives at your door. Postage is a flat $12 anywhere in New Zealand. If you would like someone to sign for it on delivery, choose signature delivery in the form for $3 extra.

Do you ship outside New Zealand?

Not at the moment. We're based in New Zealand and only supply within New Zealand. If that changes, we'll say so here.

Can I order one as a gift or memorial?

Yes. Tell us who it's for and the date you need it by, and we'll let you know if we can make it in time. For a memorial, there's no rush, and we're happy to help you choose the right photo.
