Get a quote How it works

Plain on the shelf. A photo when the light is on.

A lithophane is a plate of white plastic made at many different thicknesses. Where it is thin, light passes through and the picture looks bright. Where it is thick, less light gets through and it looks dark.

Put an LED behind it and that shading becomes your photo, with a depth you don't get from a print.

From your phone to your shelf in three steps

  1. Send your photo

    Pick one you love: a portrait, a pet, a place. Sharp photos with clear light and shade give the best detail.

  2. We make it

    Your photo is turned into a map of thicknesses and made into white plastic piece by piece.

  3. Switch on a light

    Put the tower over the light and the photo appears. Switch the light off and it goes back to plain white.

For the ones you miss

A lithophane is a quiet way to keep a person or a pet close. Choose the photo that shows who they were, and we'll turn it into a piece you can switch on whenever you want to see them.

Add glow-in-the-dark filament and the piece keeps a soft glow in the room after the light goes off. Plain white is the standard.

Questions

Which photos work best?

Sharp photos with clear light and dark areas. Portraits with the face turned toward the camera, pets and landscapes all work well. Very dark or blurry photos lose detail. If you're unsure, send it anyway and we'll tell you honestly how it will look.

Do I need to buy a light?

Not with the complete lithophane. It comes with a wooden base and a 3W LED puck light. If you order the tower on its own, place it over any steady LED light. Avoid hot bulbs, because the plastic can soften.

How big is it?

About the size of a cereal box. It sits nicely on a shelf or desk, and looks lovely with any home decor.

Why does it look blank when the light is off?

The photo is stored as changes in thickness, so it only shows when light passes through. Unlit, you'll see faint ripples and shading in the white plastic.

What's the difference between glow-in-the-dark and plain white?

Both show your photo when the light is on. Glow-in-the-dark filament also gives off a soft glow in a dark room after the light has been on. Glow-in-the-dark adds $10.

Can I order one as a gift or memorial?

Yes. Tell us who it's for and the date you need it by, and we'll let you know if we can make it in time. For a memorial, there's no rush, and we're happy to help you choose the right photo.
